How it Works

A simple 4-step process to reserve what you need.

Choose item

Browse inventory and select the item you need.

Select date

Pick the date and duration for your booking.

Get approval

Admin reviews and approves your request.

Collect & return

Collect the item and return when done.

FAQ

Who can register?

Only students/users can register via the public form. Admin accounts must be created by an existing admin or in the database.

How long can I borrow an item?

Borrowing duration depends on item and approval — see the booking form for limits.

What happens if an item is damaged?

Report damage to admin immediately. Policies on damage and replacement are handled by the admin team.
